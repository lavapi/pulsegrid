# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place for everyone who uses it or contributes to it.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussions on topic and constructive.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or offensive comments about anyone's identity or background.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Where it applies

In the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers through the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code, and will explain their decision where it's safe to.
